SMART SHOP CREDIT TRACKER · ACADEMIC PROJECT REVIEW

01 / Executive summary

A focused solution for everyday shop credit

The need

Paper or scattered records make it harder to find customer history, reconcile part-payments and prepare dues summaries.

The product

A staff-facing web app to maintain customers, record purchases and repayments, and review ledgers and reports.

Review verdict

Strong academic concept with thoughtful transaction safeguards. Production readiness is not established.

One customer record → every transaction saved → balance calculated from the ledger

Scope: academic/local-development application; no claim of live shop deployment.

02 / Problem & proposal

Replace manual reconciliation with a shared record

TODAY’S PAIN POINTS

  • Searching notebooks takes time.
  • Partial payments are easy to miscalculate.
  • Records can be missed or duplicated.
  • Current dues are hard to summarize.

PROPOSED APPROACH

  • Central customer directory and search.
  • Separate credit and payment entries.
  • Server-checked rules and current balance.
  • Ledger, pending dues and date-range reports.
Goal: improve visibility and consistency—not claim that data-entry errors disappear.

07 / Validation & safety

Useful safeguards are built into the transaction path

Amount & date validation

Positive values, at most two decimals; future transaction dates rejected.

Overpayment prevention

Backend checks current due and rejects a payment above the outstanding balance.

Concurrent writes

Customer row lock and transaction serialize balance checks for that customer.

Duplicate retry protection

Unique payment request key prevents a repeated submission being recorded twice.

Also present: BCrypt passwords · authenticated sessions · CSRF token · parameterized SQL

10 / Critical review

Good foundation—with clear next steps

CURRENT LIMITATIONS

  • Payments are not allocated to specific purchases.
  • Oldest-credit age is therefore an estimate.
  • No detailed audit/correction workflow.
  • No password reset or staff-admin screen.
  • No backup, deployment or multi-shop operations.

RECOMMENDED ROADMAP

  • Allocate repayments to credit invoices.
  • Add auditable corrections and staff roles.
  • Add tested backup and restore procedures.
  • Run integration, security and usability tests.
  • Deploy behind HTTPS with production settings.
Recommendation: present and extend as an academic prototype; do not use for real financial operations as-is.
